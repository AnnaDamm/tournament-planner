# Tourny documentation

The complete Tourny documentation is part of the application and is available in English and German.

## [Open the documentation →](https://annadamm.github.io/tournament-planner/docs/)

The documentation follows your browser language and remains available offline after Tourny has been loaded successfully.

You can also [open Tourny](https://annadamm.github.io/tournament-planner/) or return to the [source repository](https://github.com/AnnaDamm/tournament-planner).
